Inception

My Rating: ⭐ ⭐ ⭐ ⭐ ⭐

A Short Synopsis

Inception is a sci-fi thriller about a skilled thief who enters people's dreams to steal secrets, but must plant an idea instead to clear his name.

Core Plot

  • The Extractor: Dom Cobb (Leonardo DiCaprio) steals corporate secrets by infiltrating shared dream states.
  • The Impossible Job: A powerful businessman named Saito offers Cobb a chance to erase his criminal record.
  • The Twist: Instead of stealing an idea (extraction), Cobb must perform "inception"—planting a new idea into a rival corporate heir's mind.

The Mission

  • The Crew: Cobb builds a team including an architect, a chemist, and a forger.
  • Layered Dreams: They descend three deep layers into the target's subconscious during a long flight.
  • Inner Conflict: Cobb is haunted by violent guilt-ridden projections of his dead wife, Mal, who threatens the entire mission.

What I Liked

  1. Mind Bending Concepts: Dream within a dream, Puzzle like storytelling or a playful heist format
  2. Stunning Visuals and Practical Effects: The folding City, Zero Gravity hallway fight or Practical stunts
  3. The Iconic Ending: The spinning top or the endless discussion

Things I didn't like

I didn't find anything that I dislike about this film. However, many people won't understand the film, I can't really enjoy the film with my friends.

My Favourite Characters

  • Dom Cobb: Anchoring Inception with his heavy emotional core and haunting guilt.
  • Eames: The suave, quick-witted forger who steals scenes with dry humor, great banter, and iconic lines like "You mustn't be afraid to dream a little bigger, darling."
  • Arthur: The ultra-reliable, sharp-dressed second-in-command who keeps the mission on track (and delivers the legendary zero-gravity hallway fight)
  • Mal: The ultimate psychological femme fatale, serving as the terrifying and tragic projection of Cobb's subconscious guilt.
  • Ariadne: The brilliant architect who acts as the audience's surrogate, questioning the rules of the dream world so we can learn them too.
