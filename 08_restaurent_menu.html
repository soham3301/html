Starters

    • Samosa
    • Triangular, crisp fried pastries filled with a savory mixture of spiced potatoes, peas, and herbs, typically served with sweet tamarind and spicy mint chutneys.
    • Rs:- 20/-
    • Paneer Tikka
    • Cubes of firm cottage cheese, bell peppers, and onions marinated in a yogurt blend of tandoori spices, then skewered and grilled to smoky perfection in a clay oven.
    • Rs:- 80/-
    • Chicken 65
    • A fiery, deep-fried appetizer originating from South India, featuring bite-sized chicken pieces marinated in ginger, garlic, cayenne pepper, and curry leaves.
    • Rs:- 130/-

Main Course

    • Butter Chicken
    • Tender pieces of chicken are marinated in spices and yogurt, roasted, and then simmered in a rich, velvety tomato and butter sauce.
    • Rs:- 270/-
    • Paneer Butter Masala
    • Soft cubes of paneer (Indian cottage cheese) are cooked in a smooth, spiced tomato, onion, and butter gravy. It is the vegetarian equivalent of Butter Chicken
    • Rs:- 220/-
    • Chicken Biriyani
    • Fragrant basmati rice is slow-cooked with tender, spiced meat—usually chicken—using a blend of aromatic whole spices like cardamom, cloves, and cinnamon.
    • Rs:- 300/-

Desserts

    • Gulaab Jamun
    • Soft, deep-fried dough balls made from milk solids, soaked warm in a sweet rose and cardamom syrup.
    • Rs:- 10/-
    • Ras Malai
    • Tender paneer (fresh cottage cheese) discs soaked in a thick, sweetened, and chilled cream sauce flavored with saffron and pistachios.
    • Rs:- 30/-
    • Kulfi
    • Traditional Indian ice cream made by slow-simmering milk until it thickens, then freezing it with nuts and spices like cardamom or mango.
    • Rs:- 20/-

Drinks

    • Mango Lassi
    • A creamy, refreshing yogurt-based drink blended with sweet mango pulp, sugar, and often a touch of cardamom. It serves as a perfect cooling balance to spicy dishes.
    • Rs:- 50/-
    • Masala Chai
    • A comforting, aromatic black tea brewed with milk, sugar, and a blend of crushed spices like ginger, cardamom, cloves, and black pepper.
    • Rs:- 15/-
    • Nimbu Pani
    • A refreshing Indian-style lemonade made with fresh lime juice, water, sugar, and a pinch of black salt and roasted cumin powder, giving it a unique sweet and savory twist.
    • Rs:- 25/-
