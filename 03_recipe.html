Chocolate Cake

A rich, sweet baked dessert flavored with melted chocolate or cocoa powder

Ingredients

  • All Purpose Flour
  • Granulated Sugar
  • Unsweetened Cocoa Powder
  • Baking Powder and Baking Soda
  • Salt
  • Large Eggs
  • Milk and Buttermilk
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Vanilla Extract
  • Boiling Water and Hot Coffee

Cooking Steps

  • Prep Time: 15 Minutes
  • Bake Time: 30 - 35 Minutes
  • Oven Temp: 180°C (350°F)
  1. Prep the Oven & Pans: Preheat your oven to 180°C (350°F). Grease your cake pans and line the bottoms with parchment paper.
  2. Mix Dry Ingredients: Sift together the flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a large mixing bowl. Stir well to combine.
  3. Add Wet Ingredients: Add the eggs, milk, vegetable oil, and vanilla extract directly to the dry mixture. Whisk or beat on medium speed for about 2 minutes until completely smooth.
  4. The Secret Step (Thin the Batter): Gently stir in 1 cup of boiling water or hot coffee. Note: The batter will be very thin and runny at this stage, which is exactly what you want for a super moist cake.
  5. Bake: Pour the batter evenly into your prepared pans. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  6. Cool: Allow the cakes to cool in their pans for 10 minutes, then transfer them to a wire rack to cool completely before frosting.

Serving Information

A standard serving of chocolate cake is one slice (about 80g to 100g, or 1/12 to 1/8 of a standard cake), which contains roughly 350 to 390 calories.

  • Portion Size:Cut a 9-inch cake into 12 equal slices to keep portions near the standard 95g size.
  • Pairings: Serve at room temperature with a glass of cold milk, black coffee, or a scoop of vanilla ice cream.
  • Storage: Keep leftover cake covered at room temperature for up to 2 days, or refrigerate for up to 5 days.
